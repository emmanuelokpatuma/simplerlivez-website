Common questions

Quick answers

Are the apps free?

RoutineBuddy is free for carers and support workers. Dietary Insight is free to download with 3 scans a day, and every new user gets 7 days of Premium. Premium is £3.99 a month.

How do I report a bug?

Email hello@simplerlivez.com with the app name, your phone model and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

How do I cancel Premium?

Subscriptions are managed by your app store. On iPhone go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android open Google Play → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Can I ask you to delete my data?

Yes. Email hello@simplerlivez.com from the address linked to your account. Our privacy policy explains your rights.

Do you offer ongoing consulting support?

Yes, for follow-up questions, reviews or a handover to your in-house team.
