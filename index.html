FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Dietary Insight free?

Yes. Every new user gets 7 days of Premium free. After that you keep the free plan with 3 AI scans a day, plus allergen alerts, key nutrients and a daily health summary. Premium is £3.99 a month.

What does Premium include?

Unlimited scans, harmful-substance alerts, healthier swaps, food comparison, net balance, Apple Health and Health Connect sync, and no ads.

Is my data private?

We only use your data to run the app and give you your results. You can ask us to delete your data at any time by emailing hello@simplerlivez.com. Our privacy policy explains everything in plain English.

Which devices are supported?

Dietary Insight is available for iPhone on the App Store and for Android phones on Google Play.

Is Dietary Insight medical advice?

No. It gives helpful estimates and general guidance to support everyday choices. Always follow advice from your GP, diabetes nurse or dietitian, and check food labels if you have an allergy.
